Thanks for joining your child on this lemonade journey. The app is built to help kids think like small business owners: plan, price, serve, track money, and learn from the results.

  • 💛 Let them take the lead, even if the process gets messy.
  • 🧠 Talk through what they learned about price, profit, and customers.
  • 🎉 Celebrate their effort, not just the money they make.

Try practicing greetings, brainstorming flyer ideas together, and encouraging them to reflect afterward.

Mistakes are part of the lesson. The goal is to help them build confidence with real-world money skills.

You’re not just supporting a lemonade stand. You’re helping a future business owner learn by doing.
